Starter Stat Targets (All Variants)

  • Critical Strike Chance: 30-40%
  • Essence Regeneration: 10-12 (minimum - non-negotiable)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: >18%
  • Attack Speed: >50% (with Bone Prison active)
  • Maximum Life: 500+
  • Armor: Capped or near-capped (70%)

Variant-Specific Differences (Starter)

  • Damage Variant: Prioritize crit rolls on rings/amulet, use Aldkin mercenary for +Crit
  • Balanced Variant: Even split between offense/defense, use Brandir mercenary
  • Tank Variant: Stack Maximum Life and Armor rolls, use Dehuain mercenary for armor scaling
  • Push Variant: Already optimized - same gear as Damage, but with future upgrades in mind

Starter Acquisition Strategy

  • PRIORITY #1: Farm/gamble Kessime's Legacy pants FIRST
  • PRIORITY #2: Collect all mandatory UNIQUE pieces (9 total uniques)
  • PRIORITY #3: Get proper aspects imprinted on gear
  • PRIORITY #4: Select variant-aligned mercenary (Aldkin/Brandir/Dehuain)
  • Don't worry about perfect rolls - just get the pieces collected first
  • Kessime's Legacy creates power spike - your damage effectively doubles when equipped

Push-Specific Gear Mechanics

  • Block Chance Stacking: If available on gear (certain unique setups), aim for 70%+ block chance combined with Bone Armor for near-immunity windows
  • Lucky Hit Scaling: With 99-100% Crit + high Attack Speed, Lucky Hit becomes primary resource generator
  • Overpower Setup: Stack Overpower damage on gear and use Blood Lance for burst phases
  • Mercenary Optimization: Use Aldkin (Hired) + Warriors (Reinforcement) for +Crit + Guard scaling in push attempts
  • Temper Stacking: Apply 5-7 different temper effects across all gear for cumulative scaling

Push Farming Ruthlessness

  • Expect 50-100 hour farming cycles between tier upgrades at T13+
  • Weapon farming is 80% of your time at this stage
  • Join group Nightmare farming for faster loot rotation
  • Track every drop - know exactly which pieces are bottlenecks
  • Ancient Ancient gear ONLY - Rare/Uncommon quality is disenchanted immediately
  • Perfect rolls only - "close enough" costs you 20%+ damage potential

Push Mechanics & Strategies

Tier 13+ Survival Mechanics, Resolve Stacking, and Item Combinations

What This Page Covers: Pushing requires understanding synergistic mechanics beyond raw stats. This guide explains how to stack defensive layers, use Resolve mechanics, leverage corpse abilities, and combine gear/skills for maximum survival in high-tier dungeons.

Core Principle: A dead Necromancer does zero damage. Survival IS damage at tier 13+.

🛡️ SURVIVAL LAYERS STACKING

Blood Wave Necromancer has 5 defensive layers. Stack ALL of them for maximum survival:

Layer 1: Armor & Damage Reduction (Foundation)

  • Target: 95-100% Armor cap + 10-15% additional Damage Reduction on gear
  • Gear Sources: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots - all should roll Armor
  • Effect: Reduces incoming physical damage by ~95%. This is your PRIMARY defense.
  • Push Tip: Armor is non-negotiable at T13+ - every point matters

Layer 2: Maximum Life Pool (Health Buffer)

  • Target: 1400+ Maximum Life
  • Gear Sources: Pants (Kessime's Legacy), Chest, Seal, all defensive rolls
  • Synergies: Combines with Armor for effective health calculation
  • Formula: Effective Life = Max Life / (1 - Armor Reduction %)
  • Push Tip: 1400 life with 95% armor ≈ 28,000 effective health

Layer 3: Bone Armor Active Skills (Cooldown Reduction)

  • Effect: 5 ranks in Bone Armor provides periodic shields
  • Duration: ~8 second cooldown with optimal CDR stacking
  • Synergy: Pairs with Iron Maiden for revenge damage during windows
  • Push Tip: Use Bone Armor + Blood Mist together for escape sequences

Layer 4: Blood Mist Teleport (Escape Mechanic)

  • Cooldown: 4-6 second teleport available constantly with glyph support
  • Corpse Generation: Generates corpses on exit for sustain
  • Positioning: Use Blood Mist to kite dangerous champion packs away from spawn
  • Push Tip: Never stay in one spot for >3 seconds at T13+

Layer 5: Resolve Stacking (Immunity Windows)

  • What is Resolve? Stack-based immunity mechanic - each stack = 5% damage reduction up to 100%
  • How to Stack: Land Critical Strikes to build stacks (Imbiber board + high crit chance)
  • Duration: Stacks last 10 seconds with refresh on new crits
  • Maximum Stacks: 20 stacks = 100% damage immunity window
  • Push Tip: With 95%+ crit chance at high attack speed, you should have 100% resolve uptime

💀 CORPSE TENDRIL MECHANICS & SYNERGIES

Corpse Tendrils are automated crowd control - they enable push survival at tier 13+:

How Corpse Tendrils Work

  • Trigger: Activated when corpses are consumed by Blood Mist or Sever
  • Effect: Pulls nearby enemies toward corpse location, stunning them
  • Duration: Stun duration scales with skill ranks in corpse-consuming skills
  • Radius: ~40 yard effect radius - covers entire push corridor
  • Damage: Corpse Tendrils deal additional damage if Gravekeeper board is active

Corpse Generation Strategy

  • Generators: Reap, Sever, Blood Mist ALL generate corpses
  • Sever Priority: Sever should be your primary corpse generator (5 ranks)
  • Corpse Pool: Maintain 2-3 corpses in your pool for emergency Blood Mist escapes
  • Push Playstyle: Generate corpse → consume for stun → Blood Wave while enemies stunned

Item Combinations for Corpse Synergy

  • Helm Aspect (Hardened Bones): +Corpse generation from Blood Wave hits
  • Gravekeeper Board Activation: Unlocks Corpse Tendril damage multiplier (Level 50+ glyph)
  • Blood Lance Skill (3 ranks): Pairs with Overpower stacks to consume corpses for burst
  • Synergy Result: Every other Blood Wave spawns a Corpse Tendril stun
