Gear Progression Guide

Diablo IV Season 15 - Hell's Legacy

Focus: Survival, Resource Management & Damage Foundations

Key Goal: Acquire Kessime's Legacy (UNIQUE) to unlock the build's true potential

Torment Range: 1-4 | Gear Type: Mix of Legendaries + Key Uniques

Critical Notes

  • Kessime's Legacy (UNIQUE) is the MANDATORY power spike - doubles your damage
  • MUST HAVE these Uniques: Hardened Bones, Juggernaut's, Rathma's Chosen, Tidal, Deluge, Tides Blood, Crushing, Coalesced Blood
  • Aspect imprinting matters! Get the right aspects on your gear pieces early
  • Essence Regeneration + Resource Cost Reduction are MANDATORY for smooth gameplay
  • Gear rolls matter - prioritize base stat rolls over perfect rolls at this stage

Starter Stat Targets

  • Critical Strike Chance: 30-40%
  • Essence Regeneration: 10-12 (minimum - non-negotiable)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: >18%
  • Attack Speed: >50% (with Ferocity stacks)
  • Maximum Life: 500+
  • Armor: Capped or near-capped

Starter Acquisition Strategy

  • PRIORITY #1: Farm/gamble Kessime's Legacy pants first
  • PRIORITY #2: Get all mandatory UNIQUE pieces (listed above)
  • PRIORITY #3: Get proper aspects imprinted on helm/chest
  • Farm from dungeons or gamble for uniques you don't have yet
  • Don't worry about perfect rolls yet - base stats matter more at starter
  • Once you have Kessime's Legacy, the build's power multiplies significantly
  • 3x "Lucky Hit: Restore Resource" tempers on jewelry are important later

Focus: Optimizing Rolls & Aspect Synergy

Key Goal: Upgrade to better rolls on existing uniques + add tempers

Torment Range: 5-8 | Gear Status: All Uniques/Mythics obtained - now optimize rolls

Midgame Strategy

  • All mandatory uniques should be acquired - now focus on roll quality
  • Aspect imprinting: Ensure correct aspects on all pieces
  • UPGRADE ROLLS: Farm better versions with higher stat values
  • 3x "Lucky Hit Chance: Restore Resource" tempers on jewelry are MANDATORY starting here
  • Start temper farming to get +2 versions of unique items
