Complete Build Guide

Diablo IV Season 15 - Hell's Legacy

STARTER Gear (Torment 1-4)

Focus: Survival, Resource Management & Acquiring Mandatory Uniques

Critical Requirements

  • ALL of these UNIQUE pieces are MANDATORY:
  • Kessime's Legacy (Pants) • Hardened Bones (Helm) • Juggernaut's (Chest)
  • Rathma's Chosen (Gloves) • Tidal (Boots) • Thickened Blood (Mythic Amulet)
  • Deluge (Left Ring) • Tides Blood (Right Ring) • Crushing (Mainhand) • Coalesced Blood (Offhand)

Starter Gear Priorities

Get all mandatory UNIQUE pieces. Rolls matter less - focus on acquisition.

  • Essence Regeneration: 10-12 minimum (non-negotiable)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: >18%
  • Maximum Life: 500+
  • Armor: Capped or near-capped
  • Critical Strike Chance: 30-40%

MIDGAME Gear (Torment 5-8)

Focus: Upgrading Rolls on Uniques + Applying Tempers

Midgame Upgrades

  • Jewelry: Apply 3x "Lucky Hit: Restore Resource" tempers (MANDATORY)
  • All Pieces: Farm for +2 upgraded versions with better rolls
  • Helm/Chest: Upgrade Essence Regeneration rolls (maintain 10-12)
  • Weapons: Upgrade mainhand weapon damage (direct DPS increase)
  • Goal: 60-70% Critical Strike Chance total

Midgame Strategy

  • Farm Tempers aggressively - get 3x "Lucky Hit" on all jewelry
  • Weapon damage upgrades = direct DPS increase (PRIORITIZE)
  • Don't use mediocre pieces - farm until you get better rolls
  • Target farm Nightmare Dungeons for +2 upgraded gear

ENDGAME Gear (Torment 9-12)

Focus: Perfect Rolls Across All Pieces

Endgame Requirements

  • All pieces: HIGH/PERFECT rolls only - no low-roll pieces
  • Essence Regen: 12+ on helm and chest (high rolls)
  • Critical Strike: 80-90% total
  • Armor: Capped
  • Weapon Damage: MAXIMIZED on Crushing
  • Maximum Life: 1000+

PUSH Gear (Torment 13+)

Focus: Absolute Perfection in Every Stat

Push Requirements

  • 💎 ONLY Ancient/Perfect quality pieces
  • 🎲 ONLY maximum or near-maximum rolls on every stat
  • ⚔ Every temper slot optimized
  • 🔥 Weapon damage is sacred - farm relentlessly
  • Critical Strike Chance: 95-100% (MANDATORY)

Skills & Rotation

Blood Wave Necromancer is a ONE BUTTON playstyle - but here's how to optimize it

Core Concept

  • You can literally just press Blood Wave and nothing else
  • But there are key things you can do to improve your rotation
  • Focus on teleporting and positioning rather than complex rotations

Core Skills

⚔ Blood Wave
MAIN DAMAGE ABILITY (Core Skill)
Your primary damage source. With Kessime's Legacy, creates TWO waves from both sides. Spam this constantly.
  • Primary: Spam this ability - it's your entire damage
  • With Kessime's Legacy: Doubles damage, creates two waves
  • Pulls everything: Automatically pulls monsters in
  • True one-button playstyle: You can literally just press this
🌀 Sever
TELEPORT (Reaper Skill)
Teleport to a targeted location and generate a corpse. Essential for mobility and corpse generation.
  • Primary Use: Teleport around to position yourself
  • Corpse Gen: Creates a corpse for Blood Mist
  • Spam: Use frequently to stay mobile
  • Rotation: Sever → Blood Mist → repeat for teleporting
🩸 Blood Mist
CORPSE CONSUMER (Blood Skill)
Consume corpses to teleport and activate Flesh Eater legend node. Gives another teleport charge.
  • Corpse Consumption: Consumes corpse generated by Sever
  • Teleport: Teleports you like Sever
  • Flesh Eater: Activates Flesh Eater legendary node
  • Rotation: Alternate between Sever and Blood Mist for constant teleporting
⚡ Blood Lance
OVERPOWER CONSUMER (Blood Skill)
Cast from range to consume OP stacks and trigger Overpower modifier for Blood Wave.
  • OP Consumption: Spend OP stacks to trigger Overpower
  • Blood Wave Modifier: Overpower-modified Blood Wave gets OP stacks back
  • Usage: Cast when you need to spend OP stacks to gain them back
  • Bloodbath Node: Activates multiplier from Bloodbath legendary node
🦴 Bone Prison
ATTACK SPEED BOOST (Bone Skill)
Gain extra attack speed. You might not need it for T12 one-shots, but essential for tankier targets.
  • Attack Speed: Increases your attack speed significantly
  • DPS Boost: Noticeably increases damage output
  • Cap 2 Attack Speed: You get lots from this build
  • Optional: Can skip if everything dies to Blood Wave
☠ Iron Maiden
AUTOMATED (Bone Skill)
Automatically triggered - you don't need to manually cast it. Teb rune automates the casting.
  • Automated: Teb rune automates Iron Maiden application
  • Vulnerability: Applies vulnerable debuff
  • Orb Consumption: Automatically consumes orbs
  • No Action Required: Just let it run
🪦 Reap
RESOURCE RECOVERY (Bone Skill)
Occasionally weave this in if your resources are depleted to recover essence.
  • Emergency Use: Cast when resources are low
  • Essence Recovery: Gets your essence back
  • Not Primary: You shouldn't need this often if geared well
  • Optional: Only if you're starved for resources

Optimal Rotation

  • Main Rotation: Spam Sever/Blood Mist to teleport
  • Damage: Spam Blood Wave
  • Complex Rotation: Sever → Blood Mist → Blood Wave (repeat)
  • Advanced: Occasionally cast Blood Lance to consume OP stacks
  • Survival: Cast Bone Prison if needed for tankier targets
  • Emergency: Use Reap if resources get low

Teleport Rotation (Mobility)

  • Spam Sever: Teleport around map, creates corpse
  • Blood Mist comes off cooldown: Consume corpse, teleport again
  • Repeat: Rotate between Sever and Blood Mist for constant mobility
  • Result: You're constantly teleporting around, pulling everything

Paragon Boards & Glyphs

Progression through each board type and which glyphs to prioritize

Critical Note

  • Must level Essence Glyph to 50 FIRST before you can activate Essence board
  • This gates your progression - prioritize Essence early
  • After that, follow the board order below

Board Progression Order

1. Dominate (Start here)
Glyph: Dominate (increases damage from your skills)
Focus: Attack speed, critical damage, raw damage
2. Essence (After Dominate)
Glyph: Essence (life and utility)
IMPORTANT: Must level this glyph to 50 to activate Essence board slot
Focus: Health nodes, survivability
3. Gravekeeper
Glyph: Gravekeeper (corpse and bone skill synergy)
Focus: Corpse generation, bone skill damage
4. Imbiber
Glyph: Imbiber (blood skill synergy)
Focus: Blood skill damage, healing
5. Corporeal
Glyph: Corporeal (utility and scaling)
Focus: Damage scaling, final optimizations

Glyph Priority by Stage

  • Starter: Focus on Dominate glyph - raw damage
  • Midgame: Essence to 50+ (unlock Essence board), then level other glyphs
  • Endgame: All glyphs at 50+, focus on board tile selection
  • Push: Min-max every node, aim for maximum glyph levels

Glyph Priority for Blood Wave

Dominate: Increases damage from your skills (Blood Wave damage boost)
Priority: HIGHEST - direct damage multiplier
Bloodbath: Synergizes with Blood Wave Overpower modifier
Priority: HIGH - part of your core rotation
Essence: Provides life and scaling
Priority: HIGH - survivability and general scaling
Gravekeeper: Corpse and bone skill synergy
Priority: MEDIUM - supports teleport rotation
Imbiber: Blood skill synergy
Priority: MEDIUM - supports Blood Wave and Blood skills

Board Tile Selection Tips

  • Life nodes: Always take life nodes when available
  • Attack speed: Highly valuable for Blood Wave spam
  • Critical Damage: Multiplier for your damage output
  • Skill damage: Look for "Blood Skill Damage" or general damage nodes
  • Avoid resistances: Only if you're capped already

Progression Strategy

Starter/Midgame:

  • Dominate board first (direct damage)
  • Level Essence glyph to 50 (unlock board)
  • Get basic life nodes from Essence board
  • Focus on raw damage increases

Endgame:

  • All boards unlocked
  • Level all glyphs to 50+
  • Pick highest value nodes from each board
  • Prioritize life nodes for survivability

Push:

  • Min-max every node
  • Aim for maximum glyph levels (100+)
  • Every 1% damage matters
  • Every 10 life points matters
