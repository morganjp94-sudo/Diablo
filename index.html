STARTER Gear (Torment 1-4)

Focus: Survival, Resource Management & Acquiring Mandatory Uniques

Key Goal: Get all UNIQUE pieces + acquire Kessime's Legacy power spike

Critical Requirements

  • ⚠️ ALL of these UNIQUE pieces are MANDATORY:
  • Hardened Bones (Helm) • Juggernaut's (Chest) • Rathma's Chosen (Gloves)

  • Deluge (Left Ring) • Tides Blood (Right Ring) • Crushing (Mainhand) • Coalesced Blood (Offhand)

Starter Stat Targets

  • Critical Strike Chance: 30-40%
  • Essence Regeneration: 10-12 (minimum - non-negotiable)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: >18%
  • Attack Speed: >50% (with Ferocity stacks)
  • Maximum Life: 500+
  • Armor: Capped or near-capped

Starter Acquisition Strategy

  • PRIORITY #1: Farm/gamble Kessime's Legacy pants FIRST
  • PRIORITY #2: Collect all mandatory UNIQUE pieces
  • PRIORITY #3: Get proper aspects imprinted
  • Don't worry about perfect rolls - just get the pieces
  • Once you have Kessime's Legacy, the build's power multiplies significantly

MIDGAME Gear (Torment 5-8)

Focus: Upgrading Rolls on Uniques + Applying Tempers

Key Goal: All UNIQUES obtained - now optimize ROLL QUALITY

Midgame Upgrade Strategy

  • All Pieces: Farm for +2 upgraded versions with better rolls
  • Jewelry: Apply 3x "Lucky Hit Chance: Restore Resource" tempers (MANDATORY)
  • Helm/Chest: Upgrade Essence Regeneration rolls (maintain 10-12)
  • Weapons: Upgrade mainhand weapon damage (direct DPS increase - PRIORITIZE)
  • Goal: 60-70% Critical Strike Chance total

Midgame Stat Targets

  • Critical Strike Chance: 60-70%
  • Attack Speed: 30-40%
  • Essence Regeneration: 10-12 (stable)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: 20%+
  • Maximum Life: 700+
  • 3x "Lucky Hit: Restore Resource" on jewelry (MANDATORY)

Midgame Strategy

  • Farm Tempers aggressively - 3x "Lucky Hit" on jewelry MANDATORY
  • Weapon damage upgrades = direct DPS increase (PRIORITIZE)
  • Target farm Nightmare Dungeons for +2 upgraded gear
  • Mainhand weapon is #1 farming target

ENDGAME Gear (Torment 9-12)

Focus: Perfect Rolls Across All Pieces

Key Goal: Every gear piece has OPTIMIZED rolls + tempers

Endgame Requirements

  • 🎲 All pieces need HIGH/PERFECT rolls - no low-roll pieces
  • 💎 All gear pieces should be +2 upgraded or better
  • ⚔ All aspects verified and correctly imprinted
  • 🔥 Weapon damage rolls become increasingly critical
  • Critical Strike Chance: 80-90% total

Endgame Stat Targets (T12 Ready)

  • Critical Strike Chance: 80-90%
  • Attack Speed: 50%+ (with Ferocity stacks)
  • Essence Regeneration: 12+ (stable, high rolls)
  • Resource Cost Reduction: 20%+
  • All resistances: Capped (70%)
  • Armor: Capped or overcapped
  • Maximum Life: 1000+
  • Weapon Damage: MAXIMIZED on Crushing

PUSH Gear (Torment 13+)

Focus: Absolute Perfection in Every Stat

Key Goal: Ancient/Perfect gear with MAXIMUM rolls - no compromises

Push Requirements

  • 💎 ONLY Ancient/Perfect quality pieces
  • 🎲 ONLY maximum or near-maximum rolls on every stat
  • ⚔ Every temper slot optimized - zero mediocre tempers
  • 🔥 Weapon damage is sacred - farm relentlessly for max rolls
  • Critical Strike Chance: 95-100% (MANDATORY)

Push Stat Targets (Tier 13+)

  • Critical Strike Chance: 95-100%
  • Attack Speed: 60%+ (with Ferocity stacks)
  • Essence Regeneration: 12-15 (maximize)
  • All resistances: Capped (70%)
  • Armor: Capped or overcapped
  • Damage Reduction: 10-15% (on gear)
  • Maximum Life: 1200+
  • Weapon Damage: MAXIMIZED (Ancient/Perfect)
