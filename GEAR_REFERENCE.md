**Last Updated:** 2026-09-15

## Critical Item: Kessime's Legacy

**Effect:** DOUBLES wave damage (two waves from both sides)  
**Priority:** Farm/gamble this FIRST - creates a power spike  
**Stage:** Available at Starter, mandatory across all progression

## Mythic Amulet Variants

Thickened Blood is primary choice across variants:

- **Damage Variant:** Thickened Blood (resource restoration)
- **Balanced Variant:** Thickened Blood (sustain)
- **Tank Variant:** Bloodslick or Ancestral Knowledge (alternative)
- **Push Variant:** Thickened Blood (MANDATORY)

## Charm Slots (3-5 Total)

All variants use: **+Ranks to Blood Skills**

- Maximize rank bonus for Blood Wave and Blood skills
- Stack across multiple charm slots
- Secondary priorities: Intelligence, Critical Strike Chance, Attack Speed

## Seal Requirement

**Effect:** Maximum Life + 1 Charm Slot  
**Status:** MANDATORY - non-negotiable for build scaling

## Progression Stages

### Starter (T1-4)
- **Focus:** Survival, Resource Management
- **Goal:** Collect all 10 unique pieces
- **Gear Quality:** Base unique
- **Key Stats:** 30-40% Crit, 10-12 Essence Regen, 500+ Life

### Midgame (T5-8)
- **Focus:** Upgraded rolls + temper application
- **Goal:** All uniques with +2 upgraded versions
- **Gear Quality:** Unique (+2 upgraded)
- **Key Stats:** 60-75% Crit, 11-14 Essence Regen, 700+ Life
- **Tempers:** 3x "Lucky Hit: Restore Resource" on jewelry (MANDATORY)

### Endgame (T9-12)
- **Focus:** Perfect rolls on all pieces
- **Goal:** Complete gear optimization
- **Gear Quality:** Unique (+2 upgraded, majority Ancient)
- **Key Stats:** 80-95% Crit, 13-16 Essence Regen, 900-1200+ Life

### Push (T13+)
- **Focus:** Absolute perfection
- **Goal:** Maximum rolls across all stats
- **Gear Quality:** Ancient/Perfect (+2 upgraded ONLY)
- **Key Stats:** 95-100% Crit, 14-18 Essence Regen, 1200-1400+ Life
